---
title: "Introduction"
description: "Prompt management for AI products"
---

Anpord serves your prompts the way a feature flag service serves your flags. Your application asks for a prompt by id, and you change what it receives without deploying.

A prompt in your codebase is a string in a release. Rewording it means a pull request, a review, and a deploy, so the person who knows what the prompt should say waits on the person who can ship it. Anpord keeps the text outside the binary: editing a prompt writes a version, and pointing a channel at that version is what puts it live.

```ts
const prompt = await anpord.prompts.get({ id: "support-reply" });
```
