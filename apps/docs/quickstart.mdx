Anpord serves your prompts the way a feature flag service serves your flags. Your application asks for a prompt by id, and you change what it receives without deploying.

## Why it exists

A prompt in your codebase is a string in a release. Rewording it means a pull request, a review, and a deploy, so the person who knows what the prompt should say waits on the person who can ship it.

Anpord keeps the text outside the binary. Editing a prompt writes a new version, and pointing a channel at that version is what puts it live.

## What you get

  </Card>

    Staging reads one version while customers read another, from the same prompt.
  </Card>
